Key takeaways for future multi-core Zephyr/NXP work:
Always flash from the root build/cm4_rpmsg and build/cm7_shell dirs (not per-app build dirs) when using sysbuild.
If you see “no work to do” and no “Erasing”/“Programming”/“Success” log lines, use --erase or another tool and verify artifact freshness.
When debugging a non-booting or “no output” board:
Try “hello_world” sample to verify toolchain/hardware.
Reset to a known-good branch/base.
Always clean build outputs (build/) between experiments.
Device tree overlay misconfiguration, prj.conf errors, or CMake path issues are the top causes of “no output” in dual-core Zephyr projects.
